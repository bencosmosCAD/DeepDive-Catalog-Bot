# DeepDive Catalog Assistant - Client Demo Guide

## 🚀 Quick Start
1.  **Unzip** the provided folder.
2.  Double-click **`run_client_demo.bat`**.
3.  The application will open automatically in your web browser.

## 📂 Adding Your Own Catalogs
You can test the AI with your own PDF files:
1.  Navigate to the `data` folder inside `catalog_bot`.
2.  **Paste your PDF files** there.
3.  **Refresh** the web page. The AI will automatically index the new files (this may take a few seconds per file).

## 💬 How to Use
-   **Ask Questions**: "What is the price of the Vortex mask?"
-   **Compare Items**: "Compare the specs of the XL and L fins."
-   **Extract Data**: "List all wetsuits under $200 in a table."

## 🔧 Troubleshooting
-   **"Model Not Found"**: The app automatically attempts to switch between 'Fast' (Flash) and 'Standard' (Pro) models. If it fails, check your internet connection.
-   **Browser doesn't open**: Manually visit `http://localhost:8501` in Chrome or Edge.
