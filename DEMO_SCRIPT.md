# Quick Start Guide

**Welcome to DeepDive Intelligence.**

### 1. 🔍 Search
Type any product question in the main search bar.
> *Example: "Show me Suunto dive computers with air integration."*

### 2. 💲 Check Pricing
Use the **Select Price Tier** dropdown (sidebar) to switch between retail and trade prices instantly.

### 3. 🛒 Build an Order
- Click **[Add to Cart]** on any product card.
- Go to the **Shopping Cart** (Sidebar Navigation) to review your order.
- Click **[Checkout / Export]** to get a copy-paste order summary.

### 4. 🌊 Explore Brands
Scroll to the bottom of the page to browse specific brand catalogs by clicking their logo.
